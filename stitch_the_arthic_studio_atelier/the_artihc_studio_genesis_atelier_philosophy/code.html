PROVENANCE & TERRITORY

Carved from the Deccan Traps to the Eastern Ghats.

Incepted in the shadow of the Seshachalam hills in 2018, The Arthic Studio was founded as an intentional rebellion against rapid commercial pastiche. We treat architecture as an act of non-violence against the earth: deep volumetric massing, locally quarried porphyry and stone, and courtyards that inhale the south Indian monsoon wind.

Over seven continuous years, the atelier has expanded into three distinct geographic seats—Tirupati, Bangalore, and Hyderabad—each functioning as an experimental laboratory for stone carving, lime-pozzolana chemistry, and artisanal brass casting.

FOUNDATIONAL AXIOMS

The Tectonic Trinity

Three non-negotiable architectural mandates governing every contour line, sill height, and joint junction drawn within the studio.

AXIOM I MATERIALITY

Monolithic Materiality

Direct quarry-to-datum sourcing. We bypass cosmetic veneers in favor of unyielding geological mass—Navona travertine, dry-stacked granite topography, and fumed French oak aged in local linseed oils.

AXIOM II ORIENTATION

Micro-Climatic Modulation

Passive solar choreography. We tame South India's radiant noon heat via calibrated thermal massing, deep shaded porticos, and bespoke cane jali lattices engineered to accelerate ambient convective air currents.

AXIOM III TEMPORAL RESISTANCE

Generational Longevity

We build for century spans, not lease cycles. Hand-pinned bronze joinery, chiseled water spouts, and basalt copings that accept tropical rain, human touch, and oxidation as deliberate aesthetic enhancements.
