PROTOCOLS & CLARIFICATIONS

Patron Inquiries & Engagement FAQ

Prior to initiating formal correspondence, patrons are invited to review our non-negotiable operational standards and architectural governance.

QUERY 01 / SCHEDULE

What is the standard engagement timeline?

A typical monograph spans 14 to 22 months from initial site survey and geological zoning to final micro-joinery handover. We prioritize tectonic settling, natural cured lime hydration, and precision bespoke fabrication over hurried execution.

STAGES: CONCEPT • TECTONICS • STEWARDSHIP
QUERY 02 / TYPOLOGY

Do you undertake standalone interior commissions?

Yes, selectively. Standalone haute interior architecture is considered exclusively for luxury penthouses, duplex residences, and private estates exceeding 4,000 SQ. FT where structural reconfiguration of interior light and volume is permitted.

MINIMUM THRESHOLD: 4,000 SQ. FT
QUERY 03 / MATERIALITY

Can clients supply their own materials?

We exclusively curate, test, and source stone, timber, lime, and cast metal through our dedicated tactile material archive. This absolute supply-chain discipline enables TAS to guarantee our signature 25-year structural craftsmanship warranty.
