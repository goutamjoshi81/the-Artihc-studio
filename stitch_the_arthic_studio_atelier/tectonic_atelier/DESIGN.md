## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of bespoke architectural practice and haute interior design: structural permanence, material honesty, and quiet austerity. Rooted in the regional modernism and stone heritage of southern India, the interface prioritizes stillness, disciplined proportion, and deliberate whitespace over decorative digital artifacts.

The aesthetic fuses architectural editorial design with gallery minimalism. Interactions evoke the tactile precision of handling fine architectural parchment, stone materiality, and hand-finished bronze hardware. The visual cadence is slow, uncompromising, and deeply considered, serving discerning private residential patrons and commercial visionaries who value restraint over spectacle.

## Colors

The palette is derived from raw masonry, calcified stone, and time-weathered metals:

- **Honed Travertine (`#F8F6F1`)**: The primary canvas. Non-glare, organic, and matte.
- **Atelier Cream (`#F2EFE9`)**: Surface container for elevations, modules, and spatial segmentation.
- **Monolithic Noir (`#0E0E0C`)**: High-contrast, carbon-like black for editorial headlines, structural rules, and primary text.
- **Architectural Charcoal (`#181816`)**: Secondary body copy, metadata labels, and subtle visual mass.
- **Hand-Patinated Bronze (`#A8865D`)**: Active accents, focus rings, fine rules, and focal interactions.
- **Warm Ochre (`#C4A782`)**: Tertiary accent for selected states, drawing callouts, and secondary indicators.
- **Stone Hairline (`rgba(14, 14, 12, 0.08)`)**: Structural division lines mimicking drafting guides and architectural framing.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a deliberate contrast between classical editorial humanism and utilitarian blueprint precision:

- **Headlines & Monograph Titles**: EB Garamond provides deep literary dignity, echoing classical masonry engravings and architectural folios. Delicate italics are used sparingly for location stamps, project descriptors, and curated citations.
- **Structural Text & Long-form Narrative**: Plus Jakarta Sans delivers neutral, unencumbered clarity for project narratives, spatial specifications, and editorial body sections.
- **Technical Coordinates & Micro-Caps**: Syne serves as the structural drafting font, deployed strictly in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.15em` to `0.22em`) for site markers, GPS coordinates, square footage, and project stage indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical architectural grid referencing standard architectural drafting sheets (A0–A2 formats):

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column layout on desktop (`1440px` baseline), 8-column layout on tablet (`768px`), and 4-column layout on mobile (`390px`). Columns are punctuated by stone hairline vertical guides rather than hidden channels.
- **Asymmetry & Cadence**: Large monograph spreads alternate between full-bleed photographic reveals and hyper-compressed typography blocks aligned strictly along datum lines.
- **Whitespace Disciplines**: Whitespace is structural rather than purely empty. Section separations leverage generous vertical fields (`space-xl` / `5rem` to `8rem`) to slow down user interaction and demand contemplation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects physical drop shadows and skeuomorphic blur in favor of planar tectonic layering:

- **Zero Shadows**: No diffuse blurs or box-shadow treatments are permitted across surfaces.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines**: Structural boundaries rely exclusively on `1px` borders rendered in `rgba(14, 14, 12, 0.08)` to ground elements to the spatial grid.
- **Tonal Stepping**: Depth is conveyed through subtle tonal shifts: ground canvas in Honed Travertine (`#F8F6F1`), raised interactive planes in Atelier Cream (`#F2EFE9`), and active overlays in pure white or monolithic charcoal panels.
- **Inset Datum Edges**: Focused or elevated items use an inner hairline accent in Hand-Patinated Bronze (`#A8865D`) rather than visual lift.

## Shapes

The geometry of the interface is unapologetically monolithic and chiseled:

- **Zero Radius Standard**: Base components (buttons, input fields, cards, tabs, and monograph modules) have a radius of `0px`, maintaining crisp, masonry-cut precision.
- **Controlled Micro-Fillet**: Where physical tactile containers require softening against edge artifacts (e.g., interactive chips, floating concierge drawers), a strict maximum corner radius of `2px` is applied.
- **Architectural Framing**: Rectilinear geometry is paired with micro corner tick-marks (`+` indicators) and hairline frames referencing architectural coordinate sheets.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary**: Sharp rectilinear blocks (`0px` radius) in Monolithic Noir (`#0E0E0C`) with text in Honed Travertine (`#F8F6F1`). Typography uses Syne uppercase at `0.18em` tracking. Padding is calibrated to `1.25rem` horizontal by `0.875rem` vertical.
- **Ghost / Tectonic**: Bordered in `1px` stone hairline (`rgba(14, 14, 12, 0.15)`), shifting on hover to Hand-Patinated Bronze (`#A8865D`) with an inner tracking expansion of `0.02em`.
- **Monograph Action**: Understated inline link with a continuous `1px` bronze underline resting `4px` beneath text baseline.

### Chips & Technical Stamps
- Rendered in Atelier Cream (`#F2EFE9`) with a strict `2px` corner radius.
- Height is fixed at `24px` with `8px` horizontal padding.
- Text displays technical data (e.g., `HYD · 17.3850° N`, `MARBLE SPEC 04`) set in Syne bold micro-caps.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Top and side borders are eliminated; inputs use a single bottom baseline rule in Stone Hairline (`rgba(14, 14, 12, 0.15)`).
- On focus, the baseline transitions to a crisp `1px` Hand-Patinated Bronze line without box-shadow glow.
- Labels sit persistently in uppercase micro-caps above the field in Architectural Charcoal (`#181816`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Geometric squares (`16px × 16px`) with `0px` radius.
- Unselected state uses a hairline outline.
- Selected state fills with Monolithic Noir containing an inner floating square in Hand-Patinated Bronze (`6px × 6px`).

### Architectural Monographs (Cards)
- Borderless imagery resting directly on the Honed Travertine background or framed within a `1px` stone hairline box.
- Images utilize a strict 4:5 or 16:9 aspect ratio with desaturated, natural daylight rendering.
- Metadata is arranged along the base in a two-column balance: project title in EB Garamond alongside location coordinates in Syne.

### VIP Concierge Dialogue Module
- A floating rectilinear drawer sliding from the right viewport edge, backed in Atelier Cream (`#F2EFE9`) with a monolithic vertical bronze partition rule.
- Header features atelier location dials: `TIRUPATI`, `BANGALORE`, `HYDERABAD`.
- Content area pairs quiet conversational copy with structured date, budget, and estate square footage selection matrices.
